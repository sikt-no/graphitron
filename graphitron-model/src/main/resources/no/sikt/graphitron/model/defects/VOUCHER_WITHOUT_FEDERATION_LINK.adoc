Reported at the `@issue` or `@accept`, the detail naming the type.

A voucher is handed from one subgraph to another by a router, through a federation key on `internalGraphitronVoucher` that graphitron mints. Without a federation link the schema is no subgraph, and there is neither a router nor a key.
